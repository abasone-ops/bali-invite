Everyone pays the same. It doesn't matter which house you're in or which room you get — the only thing that changes your total is how many nights you stay.

Why a range? Most of the cost is fixed whether eighteen of us come or thirty-five, so the more people who join, the less each of us pays. The lower end is a full house; the upper end is a quieter one. Budget for the top — you'll likely get some back, and we'll confirm the exact figure before anyone pays anything.

And if hardly anyone can come, the figure would climb past the top of that range — the house costs much the same whether it's half full or full. If that's where we land we'll say so plainly and give you the choice again, rather than quietly sending a bigger number.

If enough of you want the full week, we'll extend the Villa to match the House and everyone gets seven nights. Tell us what you'd prefer on the form and we'll shape it around the answers.

Where You'll Sleep

Twelve bedrooms, thirty-five beds

There's more flexibility in the rooms than the numbers suggest, and how we use it makes a real difference to what everyone pays.

If you're bringing children

Several rooms take an extra bed or two, so kids can stay in with you rather than needing a room of their own — that's the default, and it keeps costs down for everyone. Want them to have their own space? Three rooms connect directly to one next door, and we'll give those to families first. Happy for yours to bunk in with other kids their age instead? We can set up dedicated kids' rooms — tell us ages and how you feel about it on the form.

If you're travelling solo

Some of the connecting rooms work well for two or three adults sharing, at the standard per-person rate. Want a room to yourself? That's fine — it's 1.5× the rate. Not meanness, just arithmetic: most of the cost per room is fixed, so one person instead of two nudges the price up for everyone else, and the supplement evens that back out.

Or stay somewhere else entirely

You don't have to sleep at Kasianda to be part of this. There are villas and hotels all through Berawa, including a couple of places about ninety seconds' walk from the front gate. Book your own, come for the days and the parties — we'd love to have you either way. Just say so on the form, so we know to count you in for food.
